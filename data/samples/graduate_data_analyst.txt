Graduate Data Analyst
Northwind Analytics
Sydney, NSW (hybrid)

Northwind Analytics is a fictional analytics consultancy used as sample
data for this project. It works with retail, energy and financial
services clients, and is hiring a Graduate Data Analyst for its insights
team.

About the role

You will work with large transactional datasets, build and maintain
reporting pipelines, and present findings to client stakeholders who are
not technical. Most of your first year is spent in SQL and Python, with
growing responsibility for owning a client reporting stream end to end.

What you will be doing

- Write SQL queries against Postgres and Snowflake to answer client questions
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI and Tableau
- Clean, validate and reconcile data from multiple source systems
- Apply statistical methods and predictive modelling to forecast demand
- Automate recurring reporting with Python (pandas, numpy)
- Present insights to client stakeholders in plain language
- Document data definitions and maintain data quality standards

Requirements

- A completed bachelor's degree in data science, statistics, computer
  science, mathematics, economics or a related quantitative discipline
- Strong SQL, and working knowledge of Python for data analysis
- Understanding of statistics: distributions, hypothesis testing, regression
- Experience building dashboards or reports in any BI tool
- Clear written and verbal communication with non-technical audiences
- Attention to detail and a habit of checking your own work

Nice to have

- Exposure to machine learning libraries such as scikit-learn or XGBoost
- Experience with version control (Git) and Jupyter notebooks
- Familiarity with cloud data platforms (AWS, Azure or GCP)
- Prior internship or project work involving real datasets
- Interest in financial services or energy markets

What we offer

- Structured graduate program with a dedicated mentor
- Hybrid working, three days in the office
- Paid study leave and a professional development budget
- 20 days annual leave plus a wellbeing day each quarter
