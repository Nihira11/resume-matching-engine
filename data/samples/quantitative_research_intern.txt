Quantitative Research Intern
Harbour Point Trading
Sydney, NSW (on-site)

Harbour Point Trading is a fictional proprietary trading firm used as
sample data for this project. The internship runs for 10-12 weeks over
the summer and sits with the research desk.

About the role

Interns work alongside researchers on signal research for equities and
listed derivatives: forming a hypothesis, testing it against historical
market data, and presenting whether it survives out-of-sample.

What you will be doing

- Analyse large high-frequency market datasets in Python
- Build and validate statistical models of short-horizon price movement
- Backtest signals and measure their decay out-of-sample
- Study volatility behaviour and its effect on execution
- Present research findings to traders and senior researchers

Requirements

- Penultimate or final-year undergraduate, or postgraduate, in
  mathematics, statistics, physics, computer science, engineering or a
  related quantitative field
- Strong probability and statistics, including hypothesis testing and
  regression analysis
- Proficient in Python, including pandas and numpy
- Comfort with time series analysis and forecasting
- Evidence of independent quantitative work: a thesis, a competition, a
  personal project

Nice to have

- Exposure to machine learning methods such as gradient boosting or
  random forests
- Experience with SQL and large datasets
- Familiarity with options pricing or market microstructure
- Experience with version control and reproducible research

What we offer

- Paid internship with accommodation and travel support
- Mentorship from a senior researcher
- Classroom training in market making and options theory
- Return offers for strong performers

Harbour Point Trading is a fictional employer. This posting exists as
