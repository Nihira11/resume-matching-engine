Junior Machine Learning Engineer
Lantern AI
Melbourne, VIC (hybrid)

Lantern AI is a fictional product company used as sample data for this
project. It builds document-understanding features for customers in
regulated industries, and is hiring a Junior Machine Learning Engineer.

About the role

You will work with the applied ML team on features that read, classify
and summarise customer documents, taking prototypes through to something
that runs reliably in production.

What you will be doing

- Build and evaluate NLP pipelines over customer documents
- Work with large language models: prompting, evaluation, retrieval
- Measure model quality with held-out sets and human review
- Deploy models behind REST APIs and monitor them in production
- Work with engineers on data pipelines feeding the models

Requirements

- A degree in computer science, data science, mathematics or a related
  field, or equivalent project experience
- Strong Python, including at least one ML framework such as PyTorch,
  TensorFlow or scikit-learn
- Understanding of machine learning fundamentals: training, validation,
  overfitting, evaluation metrics
- Familiarity with natural language processing
- Willingness to write tests and review other people's code

Nice to have

- Experience with large language models, RAG or prompt engineering
- Exposure to vector databases or embeddings
- Docker, CI/CD, or cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
- Open-source contributions or published projects

What we offer

- Mentorship and a structured ramp-up over your first quarter
- Conference and training budget
- Hybrid working, two days in the Melbourne office
- Flexible hours
